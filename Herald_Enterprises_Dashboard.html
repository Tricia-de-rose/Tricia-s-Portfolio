About Herald Enterprises

Founded by my grandfather over four decades ago, Herald Enterprises is a family-owned Sri Lankan company specialising in international trade, imports, exports and domestic distribution.

The company has built a strong market presence through longstanding supplier relationships, commercial experience and careful financial management.

My Professional Experience

As an Accounts Assistant & Finance Analyst, I independently prepared profit and loss statements and balance sheets using Simply Accounting.

I processed invoices and payments, maintained financial records, performed reconciliations and analysed revenue, expenses and profitability to support management decisions.

About This Project

This dashboard demonstrates my accounting and financial analysis skills through interactive financial statements, profitability analysis, cash flow reporting, working capital management and business performance insights.

Working Capital Explained

Current assets include cash, receivables, inventory and other current assets. Current liabilities include trade payables, short-term borrowings and accrued liabilities.

A strong current ratio is not a substitute for cash collection. Inventory and unpaid invoices still need to be converted into cash.
